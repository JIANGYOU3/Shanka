# Flashcard Domain Language

## Project

A learner-owned container that organizes decks and imported learning materials around one learning goal or source collection.

## Material

An imported source document or text owned by a project. A material may be reused by more than one deck within that project.

## Deck

A purposeful collection of cards within one project. A deck may be manual, or it may select one or more material scopes (for example, chapters from a PDF) as its source.

## Card

The individual study unit belonging to exactly one deck. A generated card can retain a material reference and a source locator for traceability; it does not independently belong to a project.

## Material scope

The explicit selection of part or all of a material that a deck may use to create cards. A deck never implicitly consumes every material in its project.

## Legacy deck

A pre-project deck with no project association. It is presented under the per-device "未归类项目" container until the learner moves it to a project.
